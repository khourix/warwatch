# Probability model: validation report, 7-day strike-wave model (hidden from the page)

Generated by `warwatch/validate.py`. Labels: the start of each strike wave on the alert-wave feeds of Israel (34 waves, feeds complete through 2026-10-11): the day the strike-wave tell turns on after 7 days off (`tells.wave_onsets`). Only those theatres' days are in the fit. A theatre-day is positive when a wave in that theatre follows within 1 to 7 days; the 7 days after each are left out.

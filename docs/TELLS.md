# Timing tells: their record on our history

Generated by `warwatch/tells.py record` on 2026-11-02. Events: `warwatch/data/events.csv` (165 in all). A tell **fires** on a day its rule holds; an **episode** is a run of firing days after 7 quiet ones. A **hit** is an episode with an event in the same theatre within 7 days of its first day; **caught** counts events with an episode starting in the 7 days before. The rules are fixed by hand from the cases analysts cite (assessment part 1), not fitted, so this record is their first test, not a tuning set.
